About the job

Are you passionate about building cutting-edge platforms that span from low-level device software to large-scale cloud systems? Nordic Semiconductor is seeking a talented and experienced Senior Full Stack Engineer to join our product engineering team. This is a unique opportunity to contribute to a platform that enables millions of devices to work seamlessly, while solving complex technical challenges across the stack.

You will work alongside a highly skilled, collaborative team of generalists with deep experience from startups, scale-ups, and leading global technology companies. At Nordic, we develop everything from SDKs for microcontrollers to large-scale backend data processing to sophisticated custom frontend visualizations. Our culture and development process encourage you to explore and contribute across the full breadth of our technology stack, depending on your interests and expertise.

This role is pivotal in advancing a platform that supports our customers and shapes the future of IoT device reliability at scale.

You will be joining the Memfault team within Nordic Semiconductor.


Key responsibilities

Onboard as either a Frontend (React/Typescript) or Backend (Python, Kotlin, Typescript) developer
Work on the hard technical challenges that interest you while delivering value to our customers and supporting our business objectives.
Start with simple, pragmatic solutions and iterate quickly. Our engineers strive to ship meaningful work every day.
Collaborate with product, design, and other engineers to take ambiguous problems and build solutions that work for our customers.
Help bring modern development tools & best practices to the whole software engineering discipline in the IoT industry.
Have a major positive impact on our product, our customers, our culture, and our business.
